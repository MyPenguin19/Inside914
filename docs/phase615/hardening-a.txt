PHASE 6.15D / STAGE A — October 24, 2026

Confirmed defects corrected:
- Expired-link support belonged only to a later stacked stage. It now lives with the email infrastructure; messages need at least 60 seconds of remaining validity before dispatch. Provider calls have a 10-second timeout. Delivery already accepted by the provider cannot be recalled; delivery latency is not guaranteed.
- A later sent/delayed event could regress delivered status. Signed events now preserve delivery success and adverse outcomes, including duplicate and late-worker reconciliation.
- One infrastructure exception could abort a due-message batch. Rows are independently isolated; durable state remains recoverable.
- Webhook bodies were bounded only after reading. Streaming now stops at 256 KB. Authenticated unsupported event types are acknowledged without executing database work.
- Historical consent had no deadline snapshot or immutable-record guard. An additive migration captures the authoritative deadline for new consent only; existing rows deliberately remain unknown and blocked for collection review. Amounts, versions and exact accepted language cannot be updated. No production records changed.
- Draft/unrecognized terms and extended deadlines block ledger creation, server-side collection, and payment-recovery access. Existing payment outcomes may still reconcile; no historical payment is discarded. Admin pages expose the reason. There is intentionally no bypass button or fabricated reconsent.

Migration policy:
The three original Phase 6.15 migrations have already been exercised locally and are published in draft PRs. They remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Stage A adds 20261024121000_email_consent_hardening.sql immediately after A's original migration. Later stages must merge A, not cherry-pick duplicate migrations.
