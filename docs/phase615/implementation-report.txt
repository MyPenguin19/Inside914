2. A — CORE RELIABILITY
Stable logical message identity and encrypted immutable payload/private link; 10-minute
fenced worker leases; finite Resend idempotency retention handling; signed Svix events;
duplicate/out-of-order protection; provider IDs/outcomes; safe audited retry only for
known rejection. Unknown acceptance beyond the safe window requires investigation.
Failed email never reverses reservation confirmation or revokes possibly delivered links.
The existing advertiser screen shows delivery status/retry. Campaigns prominently expose
passed deadlines and existing safe extension/cancellation controls. No auto-cancellation
or auto-extension was introduced. Draft legal copy now matches the manual decision policy;
legacy consent rows/amounts are unchanged and legacy wording is archived. Future consents
store the full displayed checkbox text with their new draft version.

3. B — CUSTOMER OPERATIONS
Private-link recovery with generic responses, HMAC rate limits, encrypted delivery,
15-minute single-use verification and explicit exchange; existing valid links survive.
Simple artwork/cancellation requests on the existing private reservation page. Artwork
requests stop at deadline/production/collection locks. Admin responses use existing
advertiser pages and do not themselves cancel, approve, refund or change artwork.
Deduplicated owner alerts cover reservations, artwork, requests, deadlines, full campaign
preparation, repeated message failures and cleanup failures. Configurable owner email is
required for actual notifications. Failed owner notifications cannot recursively generate
more owner emails or prevent customer-email processing.
New multi-area carts share 30 minutes and one 15-minute extension in the final five minutes,
with a 45-minute/database deadline cap. Countdown, expiration/restart guidance and limited
session-only business-name/website preservation keep the flow understandable. Personal
contact/card data is not retained locally. No expired/occupied position is silently reclaimed.

4. C — TRANSPARENCY / INVITATIONS
Private reservation page shows factual campaign/artwork/payment/mailing progress and
expected mailing dates. Only explicitly published, existing, current files for that
booking's campaign are visible. Downloads reauthorize and issue 60-second private links.
Metadata-only, missing and superseded documents fail closed. Internal evidence notes are
not shared. Postal acceptance is not claimed as delivery to every household.
Admins can create individual invitation links from available positions, optionally preview
and explicitly send an email. No hold is created. Links expire within seven days or the
campaign deadline and use ordinary area/position/artwork/contact/card-consent checkout.
Current price and availability are checked again. Duplicate sends share one durable message.
