ADVERTISING TERMS — proposed draft 2026-10-24-draft-2
1. Purchase. You reserve the identified advertising position on a shared postcard in each selected campaign. Each campaign is independent; its agreed price, position and area appear in your confirmation. No subscription or recurring purchase is created. Distribution figures and mailing windows are estimates unless separately stated in an approved agreement. An advertising area is not a promise to deliver to every address within its boundary.
2. Payment authorization. No charge is due when you reserve. Stripe stores your payment method. You authorize one later off-session charge, up to the agreed amount for each selected campaign, only after that campaign fills and Inside914 approves production. Other campaigns do not authorize an increased amount for this campaign. Additional authentication may be required. A failed payment is not proof that a charge succeeded; follow the secure recovery instructions. Material changes to amount, campaign or billing conditions require a new explicit agreement; old consent remains auditable.
3. Deadlines and incomplete campaigns. New reservations stop at the stated deadline. An incomplete campaign requires an owner decision to cancel or propose revised dates; a background job does not itself cancel confirmed reservations. No charge occurs while a campaign remains incomplete. Inside914 will communicate the decision and any material proposed changes. [OWNER/COUNSEL: approve the response deadline, extension acceptance mechanism and cancellation entitlement; reconcile prior terms promising cancellation at the original deadline. Do not charge legacy reservations following an extension without resolving their original authorization.]
4. Artwork. You retain ownership and grant Inside914 and its contracted printer permission to store, review, prepare, print and mail the supplied artwork for the agreed campaign. You confirm necessary licenses and permissions, accurate claims, working contact information and QR destinations, and lawful content. You are responsible for substantiating your advertisements. Inside914 may reject unsuitable, unlawful or technically unusable artwork and request a replacement; review is not legal endorsement. [DECIDE replacement deadline, notice period, treatment if no acceptable replacement arrives, and refund remedy.]
5. Production changes. Requests to replace artwork are reviewed before the artwork deadline and production lock. Submitting a request does not change approved artwork. No alteration is promised once printing or mailing approval locks production. Approvals relate to a specific artwork/layout version; superseded proofs do not authorize a newer version.
6. Mailing. The advertised window is a plan, not guaranteed household delivery timing. Printer acceptance and postal acceptance are different milestones. Published documents support only the milestone they describe and are not evidence of delivery to every household. [DECIDE distribution methodology, evidence standard and response if quantity or timing changes materially.]
7. Delays and errors. Inside914 will notify affected advertisers of a material production or mailing delay or a discovered print error. [DECIDE cancellation rights, reprint/credit/refund options, claim reporting window, who bears printer fault, and obligations for undelivered/returned mail. No blanket disclaimer or automatic waiver is proposed.]
8. Disputes. Contact the published support address with the reservation reference and relevant evidence. Inside914 will investigate using the agreed terms, consent and production records. Nothing in these drafts is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be waived. RESERVATION / CANCELLATION / REFUND POLICY — proposed draft
A temporary hold is not a confirmed reservation. Its visible expiry and any limited extension govern availability; expired positions may be selected by someone else. Confirmation requires completed artwork, contact details and explicit saved-card authorization.
A customer may request cancellation through the private reservation page. A request is not immediate deletion or a guaranteed refund. Inside914 reviews the campaign, payment and production state under the accepted policy. Records are retained for audit. [DECIDE the unconditional cancellation window and response SLA.]
If the owner cancels an uncharged campaign, affected uncharged reservations are cancelled consistently and cannot later be collected. Notifications are queued independently of cancellation. A saved card shared with another active reservation remains available for that reservation; otherwise removal is retried if the provider is unavailable. Email or card-removal delays do not restore a cancelled reservation.
[DECIDE refund entitlement and timing for customer cancellation before/after charge, printer error, delayed/cancelled mailing, rejected art, partial delivery and disputes; whether processing fees may lawfully be withheld; payment method for refunds. Do not publish “no refunds” or promise automatic refunds without approval and implementation.]
Current implementation has no unrestricted customer refund button. Paid reservations require owner payment-resolution review. No new automatic refund or collection is included in Phase 6.15.
