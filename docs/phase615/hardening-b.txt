PHASE 6.15D / STAGE B

Recovery choice: bounded immediate delivery (option B), with the existing durable envelope as fallback. The request's generic response and email/IP rate limits remain. A managed Next after() callback prepares the persistent envelope and attempts delivery twice at most (10-second HTTP timeout each, one-second gap). Both calls use the exact same payload and idempotency key. Unknown first acceptance remains ambiguous even if the second call returns a definitive rejection. No automatic token regeneration.

The database serializes duplicate requests for one reservation during a one-minute window. It creates one 15-minute token, and its exact expiry is used in the encrypted email. Exchanges still lock and consume that token once. Existing valid access tokens are never revoked. A distinct later owner-requested recovery may coexist with an older valid link. A queued email with less than one minute left is not dispatched. Provider-side delay after acceptance is outside our control. When no usable email arrives, the form explicitly permits requesting another (maximum three requests per email/hour). Longer outages fail safely, retain administrative attention, and may require support after the rate limit. Daily cron is not the recovery-link delivery promise.

Important reservation and customer-request events commit durable in-app alerts transactionally. Managed after-response callbacks promptly refresh and attempt owner delivery after confirmation, signed webhooks, requests and cleanup. The existing authenticated daily job recovers interrupted callbacks and refreshes failures after processing. No new scheduler/provider was introduced. Six due owner messages per prompt drain; remaining durable messages are recovered by subsequent jobs. Hobby daily scheduling has up to 59 minutes of timing variation and can miss an entire short-lived token window; minute-level retries require a separately approved scheduling/plan decision. No guarantee of immediate inbox delivery.

Missing ADMIN_NOTIFICATION_EMAIL now displays an explicit in-app warning. Unknown/terminal failures and preparation failures create in-app attention even on the first nonretryable result. Owner delivery failures never spawn more owner emails. Concurrent jobs use stable alert identities and delivery leases. Customer reservations do not wait for owner delivery.

Request responses are explicitly described as visible on the private reservation page, not emailed. The cart timer receives the persisted extension count on refresh. Database limits remain 30 minutes + one optional extension, capped at 45 minutes and the earliest campaign deadline. An extension which would add no time is rejected without consuming the allowance.

New additive migration: 20261024131000_recovery_notification_hardening.sql, after the original B migration. Original published migrations remain unchanged.
