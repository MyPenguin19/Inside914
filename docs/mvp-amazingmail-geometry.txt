These are FILE-origin coordinates. A future integration with trim-origin
campaign slot coordinates must explicitly translate them by the bleed inset;
they must not be copied into legacy campaigns as-is.

The front grid is centered in the live rectangle. The back grid is aligned
to its left edge and centered vertically. Rows and columns meet edge-to-edge;
this candidate does not invent an inter-ad gutter or resize any paid tile.

Back live width left of postal = 5.2 - 0.1875 = 5.0125 inches.
Two paid columns use 5 inches, leaving 0.0125 inch before postal: 0.3175 mm.
That remaining width is the entire allowance for any horizontal gutter plus
extra postal clearance. A required combined allowance larger than 0.0125 inch
would invalidate this arrangement at 2.5 inches per tile.

For this two-column arrangement, the square upper bound is:
(5.0125 - column_gap - extra_postal_clearance) / 2.
At zero additional gap/clearance the bound is 2.50625 inches. This is not a
recommendation to enlarge the paid tiles or a maximum over all arrangements.
