# Inside914 — Local SEO and backlink acquisition strategy
Updated 2026-10-25. Research plan; no directories, placements, outreach or Search Console settings have been changed.

## Positioning and source of truth
- Service: shared 10-position postcard advertising in Westchester County, New York.
- Operator: Inside Diagnostics LLC. Public brand: Inside914. Contact: Info@inside914.com.
- Canonical host: https://inside914.vercel.app until a custom host is verified and redirected.
- No promise of confirmed household quantities or postal routes before printer verification; no invented testimonials, ratings or sales outcomes.
- Commercial checkout is release-gated: avoid a “Book now” promise while disabled.

## 90-day earned link plan
1. **Week 1–2 — Owned and partner foundations**: make Inside914's accurate brand/business information consistent on its social profiles and legitimate local business profiles where eligible. Check eligibility and operator/assumed-name requirements before any listing. Never invent a physical storefront address or publish the owner’s private address.
2. **Weeks 2–4 — Relevance**: identify Westchester chambers, merchant associations and professional groups serving Central, Southern, Northern and Eastern Westchester. Only join or request a member profile when eligibility and any fees are transparent and owner-approved. Focus on genuine membership, not backlinks as the sole consideration.
3. **Weeks 3–6 — Original local educational assets**: publish truthful, useful explainers on shared direct mail and postcard artwork requirements, documenting actual printer standards before making print specifications or postal claims. Ask independent partners to cite genuinely useful content voluntarily.
4. **Weeks 5–9 — Relationship outreach**: contact local print designers, chambers, small-business newsletters and business-support organizations. Suggest genuinely useful co-marketing resources or interviews, not paid followed links. Ask permission to cite partners.
5. **Weeks 8–12 — Measurement and maintenance**: review indexed pages, branded queries, organic landing pages and referring domains in Search Console/analytics. Check and correct broken URLs, duplicate references and low-quality links; do not disavow links without evidence of a harmful pattern.

## Outreach prospects and criteria
- Westchester regional chambers and recognized local merchant groups: verify current web URL, membership terms and relevance.
- Local print/design agencies that explicitly accept referral partnerships: business fit first.
- Local business education events and independent newsletters: contribute a real educational resource.
- Existing Inside914 advertiser partners: ask only if they are happy with the service and if disclosure is appropriate.
- Inside Diagnostics LLC/InsideDx: cross-link only when there is a genuinely useful user-facing connection, accurately disclose common ownership, avoid sitewide keyword-stuffed links.

## Rules
- No purchased backlinks, link farms, private blog networks, bulk directory blasts, automated forum posts or manufactured testimonials.
- Sponsorship and paid placement links must be tagged appropriately (rel=sponsored/nofollow when appropriate), in accordance with Google's link-spam policies.
- No mass outreach without approved templates, appropriate sender identification and opt-out handling.
- Track contact, source URL, audience, membership requirements, fee, owner approval, outreach date, editorial result and link destination. Do not record 'obtained' unless verified.
- Prioritize 5–10 genuinely relevant earned citations over 100 generic directory links.

## Search Console and Core Web Vitals verification
- Verify the domain/URL-prefix property through Google's supported ownership flow using an owner-controlled DNS record, HTML file or meta token; do not claim verification without confirmation.
- Submit https://inside914.vercel.app/sitemap.xml only after deploying and validating the endpoint.
- Verify canonical selected URL, indexing, coverage, and URL Inspection on public pages.
- Collect PageSpeed Insights or Search Console field-data metrics for mobile and desktop and act on measured LCP, INP and CLS. Never invent baseline scores.
- Record pre/post URLs and measurements, investigate non-SEO assets separately. Printer-resolution customer artwork should not be destructively compressed just to improve web performance.
- When moving from vercel.app to an approved custom domain, update absolute canonical URLs, sitemap, structured data, social URLs and 301 redirects as one coordinated release.
