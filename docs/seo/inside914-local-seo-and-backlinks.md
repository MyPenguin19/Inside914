## Five article briefs based on current customer questions

1. **Shared postcard or your own mailing?** Explain sharing ten paid positions versus commissioning a separate mailing, the tradeoffs in message space and coordination, and questions to ask a printer. Link to `/#how-it-works` and `/areas`. Avoid invented competitor prices or response rates.
2. **How to choose a Westchester advertising area.** Use the existing proposed municipality guide; explain service radius, store location and why an area name is not a delivery guarantee. Link to all four regional pages. Require actual routes before publishing a delivery map.
3. **Preparing artwork for your Inside914 position.** Answer file quality, crop review, legibility and contact-information questions using the current uploader and approved campaign template. Preserve original files. Final postal/print specifications require printer confirmation; do not reuse outdated V1 dimensions as universal guidance.
4. **Measuring a postcard campaign without overstating results.** Show a simple inquiry log, optional campaign code and distinct landing page. Separate mailing evidence from individual delivery or sales attribution. Use hypothetical examples explicitly labeled as examples, not fabricated customer case studies.
5. **What $0 at reservation means.** Explain saved-card consent, the displayed price for each area, independent campaign fill/approval and what happens if an area does not fill. Reference the approved policy text and current release status. Do not promise immediate booking or quote development $1 prices.

These are proposed articles, not published pages. None was added to the sitemap before implementation.
