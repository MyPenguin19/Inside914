Inside914 customer-flow simplification — local implementation, November 2, 2026

Customer journey
1. Areas: choose one or more areas from compact cards or the existing map.
2. Your ad: choose one position per area, then upload and preview artwork.
   Continue to artwork now opens the first selected area's editor directly.
   The area checklist supports separate images or copying an image for review.
3. Business details: one contact form applies to all selected areas.
4. Save card: review the area prices, give explicit consent, and reserve for $0 today.

Improvements
- Mobile area cards precede the map. Town names remain; repeated population tables
  were removed from checkout. Proposed coverage qualifications remain visible.
- Only one area's position picker is displayed at a time. Area buttons show saved
  choices, and Next guides the customer to the next unfinished area.
- Going back preserves choices. Catalog validation still prevents stale positions
  from being submitted. Network errors preserve choices for retry.
- The selection total and next action stay within reach while scrolling.
- No extra Add artwork stop immediately after holding positions. The server sets
  the first area's derived HttpOnly hold capability only after successful creation.
- Artwork cards show area, position, price, thumbnail, and readiness. Campaign
  details are optional; image-copy controls collapse once an ad is ready.
- Contact validation focuses the first invalid field without clearing other entries.
- Checkout has a compact itemized order summary and optional artwork review.
- Removed the stale Pending owner and legal review label. Approved consent text
  remains byte-identical, visible, and unticked by default.
- A network failure no longer leaves card submission stuck. If saving succeeded,
  confirmation can be retried without calling confirmSetup again.
