Implemented
- Four-zone overview, including missing-campaign links. Each card identifies its
  campaign/month and counts valid confirmed advertisers independently out of ten.
  Open campaigns take precedence; otherwise the earliest active square campaign
  is shown. Historical campaigns remain accessible in the existing selectors.
- Reservations show normalized category and all selected zones, fetched by cart
  independently of table pagination using the signed-in admin's RLS client.
- Shared readiness checklist on campaign and production pages: ten exact paid
  positions, valid reservations, unique categories/claims, approved square art,
  closed reservations, printer approval, mailing window, house proof and collection.
- Invalid, cancelled, pending, released, rejected, already charged, failed-payment,
  flagged, missing-consent/card/customer, wrong-price and duplicate category/slot
  reservations cannot qualify as valid confirmed advertisers in this checklist.
- Activate & Charge Zone is visibly disabled. No charge endpoint was implemented.
  The old print-approval UI and server action are blocked for square campaigns;
  legacy campaigns retain their prior workflow. This is a local application guard,
  not a replacement for database enforcement in the future collection phase.
- Production previews display six front/four back paid positions plus two labeled
  house-artwork placeholders and the postal exclusion, from the Phase D geometry.
  Actual house artwork has not been supplied; placeholders are not print assets.
- Artwork/reservation detail previews use 9x6 dimensions for square campaigns.
  Existing review actions, administrator authentication, RLS and audit logs remain.
- Existing components, typography, colors and page structure are reused. The
  evidence form now fits 320px by allowing its grid/file input to shrink; no global
  overflow clipping was added.
