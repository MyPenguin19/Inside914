Clearance findings and stop condition
Two back columns have 5.0125 inches of horizontal space. Two 2.5-inch squares
leave only 0.0125 inch (0.3175 mm) for any column gutter plus postal clearance.
The current candidate has zero inter-ad gutter. Its lower house region has zero
additional postal clearance. Printer acceptance must be obtained before use.
For the current two-column/two-row arrangement:
maximum square = min((5.0125 - column gap - postal clearance)/2,
                     (5.75 - row gap)/2).
With zero additional allowances, the upper bound is 2.50625 inches.
For example, a required 0.125-inch column gap lowers it to 2.44375 inches.
The new calculator reports such conflicts; it never resizes artwork or placements.
If printer requirements invalidate 2.5-inch tiles, stop for owner approval rather
than silently shrinking or distorting them. No new allowance has been assumed.
