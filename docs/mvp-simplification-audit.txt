GEOMETRY GATE — NOT VERIFIED

The owner's brief supplies 9 x 6 trim, 9.125 x 6.125 bleed file, approximately
8.75 x 5.75 live area, and a 3.925 x 3.33 lower-right postal exclusion panel.
These dimensions do not specify the panel's exact origin or required clearance,
and the live bounds are explicitly approximate. They are not enough to certify
positions or maximum safe printed square size.

Official sources inspected on 2026-10-19:
https://www.amazingmail.com/templates/blank-templates
https://www.amazingmail.com/templates/start
https://sendcards.amazingmail.com/getstarted

The public Print & Mail postcard selector lists 4.25 x 6, 5.5 x 8.5, 5.5 x 11.5,
and 3.5 x 8.5. No exact 9 x 6 landscape template was located there or in the
linked public template files. This does not establish that the product does
not exist. A direct official template link or printer-provided PDF is needed.

Conditional arithmetic, NOT printer-approved geometry:
- Six 2.5-inch squares in a 3 x 2 front grid occupy 7.5 x 5 inches, before
  gutters. That footprint fits inside the stated approximate live rectangle.
- IF live bounds are centered and IF the postal panel touches the trimmed
  right/bottom edges, the safe width to its left is 9 - 3.925 - 0.125 = 4.950
  inches. A 2 x 2 back grid of 2.5-inch squares needs 5.000 inches: 0.050 too wide.
- For that specific arrangement, 2.475 inches is an optimistic zero-gutter
  upper bound, not an approved size or a proven maximum across all arrangements.
- Under those same assumptions, space above the panel is 2.545 inches high.
  Other back arrangements and the two house ads have not been ruled out or
  verified. Their fit must be tested together against the actual template.

No placement coordinates were hardcoded; no artwork was resized or distorted.
Keep the requested 2.5 x 2.5 upload standard. Once the official template is
available, reproduce trim/bleed/live/postal bounds, measure all placements and
clearances, and calculate any required size change. A smaller printed tile
requires explicit owner approval. Both house ads need verified reserved regions
and supplied/approved artwork before any production proof can be approved.
